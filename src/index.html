Faith, conscience & kindness

Can devotion ask us to cause harm?

A compassionate question can be an act of reverence, not disrespect.

Some people are told an animal must be killed for a ritual, or that doing so guarantees divine blessing. Beliefs and interpretations differ; no single reading speaks for every believer.

Sacred writings—including the Bhagavad Gita, the Bible, the Guru Granth Sahib, and many others—are understood in different ways and within rich, diverse traditions. It is neither fair nor accurate to dismiss whole traditions as “fake” or reduce them to one text.

Ask where a claim comes from. Read it in context. Listen to thoughtful voices within the tradition—and consider the life affected by the practice.

Nourishment without harm

Protein can come from plants.

Choosing meals with plant protein is one way to nourish yourself while leaving animals off your plate. There are plenty of familiar, satisfying options to explore.

Protein needs vary with age, health, and activity. A varied, well-planned diet can help meet them; a registered dietitian or healthcare professional can offer advice for your needs.

06

Oats, quinoa, and other grains round out a varied diet.
